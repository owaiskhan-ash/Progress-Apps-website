02 / The idea

Access should not
feel like a downgrade.

Progress Apps is a simple experiment: take focused Android apps that would normally sit behind a price, and make them available to everyone.

Free does not mean filled with ads. It does not mean harvested data. It means the same considered experience, with the door left open.
